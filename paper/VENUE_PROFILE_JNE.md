## Fit

The journal explicitly covers brain-computer interfaces, neural signal
processing, innovative neurotechnology, and computational neuroscience. The
present paper is within scope as a computational SSVEP decoding and deployment
study.

The journal also states that a modest classification increase on small open
datasets is generally insufficient unless the work includes comparisons with
modern methods, validation on additional datasets, and insight into the source
of the improvement. The manuscript is organized around those three
requirements: protocol-matched modern references, four public datasets, and
separate mechanism and deployment analyses.
